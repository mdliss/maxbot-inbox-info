Privacy policy

This notice covers MaxBot private inbox, operated by Max Liss for accounts he authorizes. It does not cover other tools that may share a Google consent configuration.

Information accessed

Configured sources provide account identity, sender and conversation metadata, subjects, message text, timestamps and source links. Enabled Read AI access includes reports and transcript excerpts from meetings the operator attended. Each connection requires dedicated account authorization.

Purpose and AI processing

The inbox uses this information to identify follow-ups, commitments and deadlines, prepare briefs and apply configured alert rules. Limited message excerpts, metadata and retained summary or task context are sent to OpenAI for analysis. Requests use its store: false option; this is not a guarantee of zero retention by the provider.

Stored information

Google Cloud Firestore stores derived summaries, source links, tasks, processing checkpoints, fingerprints and delivery or completion receipts. These records can contain private information. Full raw message bodies and transcripts are not saved in inbox state. Authorized infrastructure administrators retain access to stored data.

Delivery and account boundaries

Briefs and alerts are restricted to the configured operator’s private Slack destination and own email. Slack and Google process those deliveries. Personal Gmail access is read-only; optional alert email requires separately authorized sending access. The inbox does not send replies to other people or modify source messages.

Retention and control

There is no automatic deletion period. Removing or rebinding a source clears its pending derived state on a later scan; delivery and completion receipts may remain. Revoking a provider grant stops further authorized access but does not itself erase stored summaries or receipts. Contact the operator about removing retained data.

Contact

For privacy, access or deletion questions, contact Max Liss at maxliss19@gmail.com.
