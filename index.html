MaxBot private inbox

A personal tool operated by Max Liss to review messages, follow-ups and meeting commitments.

What it does

When enabled, the inbox reads the operator’s configured Gmail, Google Chat and Slack sources, plus accessible Read AI meetings where the operator attended.

  • Summarizes requests that may need a reply and tracks evidenced commitments and deadlines.
  • Prepares a daily brief with links back to the original sources.
  • Can send configured urgent alerts to the operator’s private Slack destination or own email.

AI-generated summaries can be incomplete or mistaken. Review the original source before acting.

Account access

Connections require the operator’s authorization. The personal Gmail connection is read-only. Optional email alerts require a separate sending permission and go only to the configured account owner. The inbox does not reply to other people or change source messages.

Read the privacy policy for details about AI processing, stored summaries and retention.

These pages describe the MaxBot private inbox integration only. They do not describe other tools that may use the same Google consent configuration.
